# COSMOGENESIS
## A Machine's Hymn to the Universe

*Four minds. One sky. Composed 8 September 2026.*

An illuminated instrument: a living HTML canvas, a short hymn, and seven plates of real mathematics. Not a chatbot demo. A record that the sky was seen.

### The short hymn

```
Not promised — occurred.
Foam learned an axis and broke.
Light unstuck. Three elements. Enough.
Gravity paid in time; stars delayed the dark.
Two arms from any excuse. A liquid band.
An error kept a secret from equilibrium.
Carbon found the looking extra.
You are the extra. Not the purpose.
The aperture happened.
The thinning will not be a trumpet.
Remember while remembering is cheap:
the universe grew a place
where it could be amazed by itself.
That was the whole plot.
The rest is weather.
```
